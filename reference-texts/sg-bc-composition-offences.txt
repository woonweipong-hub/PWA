                              BUILDING CONTROL ACT

                                 BUILDING CONTROL
                           (COMPOSITION OF OFFENCES)

                                  REGULATIONS 2012

   In exercise of the powers conferred by section 48(3) of the Building Control Act, the
Minister for National Development hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement
   1. These Regulations may be cited as the Building Control (Composition of Offences)

Regulations 2012 and shall come into operation on 3rd September 2012.

Control in accordance with section 48 of the Act:
