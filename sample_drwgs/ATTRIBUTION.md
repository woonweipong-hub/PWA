# Sample Drawings — Sources & Licensing

Files in this folder are used as license-clean demo assets for the
SiteShrimp Tag / Compare / Convert features. Each file's origin, author,
and license is listed below.

## Dyckman House floor plan sketches

- `Dyckman_First_Floor_sketch.png`
- `Dyckman_Second_Floor_sketch.png`

**Source:** Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS), Library of Congress
Prints & Photographs Division — *Dyckman House, Broadway and 204th Street,
New York, New York County, NY* (HABS NY,31-NEYO,11-). Sheet 2 and Sheet 3
of 11, retrieved via Wikimedia Commons.

**License:** **Public domain.** Works produced by U.S. federal government
employees in the course of their duties (HABS is a National Park Service
program) are not subject to copyright in the United States.

- Collection: <https://www.loc.gov/pictures/collection/hh/>
- Wikimedia mirror category: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:HABS_drawings>
- LoC usage guidance: <https://www.loc.gov/rr/print/res/114_habs.html>

No attribution is legally required. We credit HABS and the Library of
Congress here as courtesy and for transparency.

## LowerFloorPlan.pdf / rac_EL_V1.* / rac_EL_V2.*

These legacy files pre-date this attribution document. `rac_EL_*` are
Autodesk's `rac_basic_sample_project` files bundled with Revit, subject
to the Revit EULA — **not cleared for public redistribution** and should
not be served to end users of SiteShrimp. `LowerFloorPlan.pdf` is of
unknown provenance. Both sets are kept locally for internal testing only
and must be reviewed / replaced before being shipped in-app.
